Meet the Baker

Hi, I'm Nancy

Baking has been one of my favorite hobbies for many years. My idea was simple: bake comfort-like desserts you don't really find at the stores — and even if you did, mine are always fresh and made to order.

I'm a mom of 4, wife of 1, and I have 4 fur babies (2 dogs, 1 cat & 1 bunny). Life is beautifully full, so I bake a couple days a week and keep my orders intentionally limited. I'm a one-woman show — and I prefer it that way.

I have my CFO baking permit & business license. My style is simple — always learning, always growing. I'm humbly grateful to be a part of your special moments through dessert.

"Community over competition is how it should be. 🤎"

I regularly spotlight other small businesses on my IG stories. It's not just me me me… it's Us. I believe we as a community need to have each other's back.
