# Nancy G's Home Bakery — Design Ideas

## Approach 1 — Warm Farmhouse Editorial

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Modern Farmhouse meets food editorial photography.

**Core Principles:**
- Warmth through earthy, creamy tones and natural textures
- Generous whitespace that lets product photography breathe
- Handwritten-style display type contrasted with clean body text
- Subtle paper-grain texture on backgrounds for a handmade feel

**Color Philosophy:** Cream (#F9F4EC) background, terracotta/rust (#C4522A) accent, deep espresso (#2D1A0E) for text, sage green (#7A9E7E) as a secondary accent. Emotional intent: warmth, comfort, and trustworthiness — the feeling of a home kitchen.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial grid. Hero section uses a large left-aligned photo with text offset to the right. Menu items are laid out in an uneven masonry-style card grid. Bio section uses a split-column with photo bleeding to the edge.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rule dividers with small floral/heart motifs
- Subtle linen-texture SVG overlay on section backgrounds
- Rounded corners only on images, sharp corners on cards

**Interaction Philosophy:** Soft, slow hover transitions (250ms ease-out). Cards lift gently on hover with a warm shadow. Smooth scroll between sections.

**Animation:** Entrance animations: fade-up at 0.4s for headings, staggered 60ms for menu cards. No jarring motion.

**Typography System:** Display: "Playfair Display" (serif, bold italic for headings). Body: "Lato" (400/600). Price labels: "Playfair Display" italic.
</text>

## Approach 2 — Latina Artisan Fiesta

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Contemporary Mexican folk art meets clean digital design.

**Core Principles:**
- Vibrant but controlled color palette inspired by papel picado and talavera
- Organic shapes and hand-drawn border motifs
- Celebration of cultural identity as a design feature
- Clean, readable layout beneath the decorative layer

**Color Philosophy:** Off-white (#FFF8F0) base, deep coral (#E8523A), marigold yellow (#F5A623), forest green (#2D6A4F). Emotional intent: pride, joy, community, and festivity — reflecting the "Proud Latina owned" identity.

**Layout Paradigm:** Centered hero with a bold full-bleed banner, then a structured two-column menu grid with decorative section dividers. Bio section uses a circular photo frame with decorative border.

**Signature Elements:**
- Papel picado-inspired SVG banner dividers between sections
- Marigold-colored price badges
- Warm gradient overlays on hero image

**Interaction Philosophy:** Playful hover states with a slight scale-up (1.03) and color pop. Menu cards use a warm glow shadow on hover.

**Animation:** Confetti-style staggered entrance for menu items. Hero text slides in from left.

**Typography System:** Display: "Abril Fatface" (bold, impactful). Body: "Nunito" (rounded, friendly). Accent labels: "Dancing Script" (handwritten feel).
</text>

## Approach 3 — Soft Pastel Cottage Bakery (CHOSEN)

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Romantic Cottagecore meets modern food blog minimalism.

**Core Principles:**
- Soft, airy palette that feels fresh and inviting
- Generous vertical rhythm with clear section hierarchy
- Elegant serif display type for warmth, clean sans-serif for readability
- Product photography as the hero — minimal UI chrome

**Color Philosophy:** Warm white (#FEFAF6) background, dusty rose (#D98B8B) primary accent, sage green (#8FAF8A) secondary, deep charcoal (#2C2C2C) for body text, soft blush (#F5DDD8) for card backgrounds. Emotional intent: cozy, handmade, trustworthy, and delicious.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-width hero with a large centered headline and a soft watercolor-style background tint. Menu section uses a clean three-column grid on desktop, single column on mobile. Bio section is a horizontal split with a rounded photo on the left and text on the right. Allergen and policy sections use a soft blush card with a left accent border.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin serif decorative dividers (em-dash ornaments)
- Soft drop shadows on product cards (no hard borders)
- Heart (🤍) motif echoing the PDF branding used sparingly as a decorative element

**Interaction Philosophy:** Gentle hover lifts on cards (translateY -4px, 200ms ease-out). CTA buttons use a warm rose fill with a subtle scale press on click.

**Animation:** Fade-up entrance for sections as they scroll into view (IntersectionObserver). Staggered 50ms delay per menu card. No motion for users who prefer reduced motion.

**Typography System:** Display: "Cormorant Garamond" (elegant serif, bold for headings). Body: "DM Sans" (clean, modern, highly readable). Price: "Cormorant Garamond" italic. All caps labels: "DM Sans" 600 tracked.
</text>
